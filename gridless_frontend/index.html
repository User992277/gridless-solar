Sector 01 — The Problem

Owning the flat doesn't mean owning your power bill.

High-rise living in the Tricity comes with a specific kind of powerlessness: the roof belongs to the RWA, the wiring belongs to the builder's plan, and every summer the grid buckles right when your AC needs it most.

01

The roof isn't yours

Rooftop solar needs society sign-off that rarely comes fast — if it comes at all.

02

Net-metering is a maze

DISCOM approvals, load studies, inspection visits — months of paperwork before a single panel goes up.

03

Summer cuts the power anyway

Peak-load afternoons mean the exact hours you need backup are the hours it's least reliable.

Why "gridless"

Off the paperwork grid. Not off the power grid.

The name is about the approval process, not the electricity. Grid-Tied kits still work with your existing connection — you just skip the net-metering queue. Hybrid kits give you a private grid of your own for the hours the real one goes down.

Every install is inspected in person before a single rupee changes hands.
